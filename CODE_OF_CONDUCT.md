# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), summarised here.

## Our pledge

We want taking part in Tuner to be free of harassment for everyone, whatever their age, body, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity or expression, experience, education, social or economic status, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and respectful, including when you disagree.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.
- Focus on what is best for the people who use Tuner.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Trolling, insults, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing other people's private information, such as addresses or account credentials, without their permission.
- Anything else that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that break this code, and may ban people temporarily or permanently. To report a problem, contact the maintainer privately through GitHub ([@nelsonmandeladev](https://github.com/nelsonmandeladev)). Reports are handled confidentially.

This code applies in all project spaces and when someone represents the project in public.
